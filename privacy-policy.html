Privacy Policy

Day Pilot: Daily Routine & Goals

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Your privacy, simply explained

Day Pilot is designed to let you record and manage your daily activities directly on your device. In the current version, your personal app entries are stored locally on your device and are not automatically uploaded to a Day Pilot server.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Day Pilot does not require you to create an account or provide your email address, phone number, name, or other personal information to use the app.

Information that you choose to enter into the app may include:

  • Daily activities and routines
  • Habits
  • Goals and milestones
  • Meal logs
  • Reminders
  • Diary or journal entries
  • Personal notes
  • Other information you choose to record

2. Your Data Is Stored on Your Device

The current version of Day Pilot does not operate its own server for storing your personal app data.

Information you enter into Day Pilot is stored locally on your device and is not automatically uploaded to a Day Pilot server.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal app data with third parties.

3. Photos and Media

Day Pilot does not upload your photos or personal media to our servers.

If a future version introduces features that require uploading or processing photos, media, or other personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the relevant data practices will be disclosed before those features are used.

4. Data Sharing

For the current version of Day Pilot, your personal entries are not shared by us with third parties.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your diary entries, notes, meal logs, goals, or other manually entered content for advertising.

5. Data Security

Because your Day Pilot data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including your device passcode and other device-level security features.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a secure screen lock.

6. Data Loss and Device Replacement

Because the current version of Day Pilot stores your data locally on your device and does not provide server-side backup, we cannot guarantee recovery of your data if you uninstall the app, reset your device, lose your device, damage your device, or otherwise lose access to the device.

If the app is uninstalled or the device is lost, deleted local data may not be recoverable.

Please do not uninstall the app if you need to retain your locally stored Day Pilot data.

7. Future Features

Day Pilot may introduce additional features in future versions, such as cloud backup, synchronization, optional accounts, analytics, subscriptions, or advertising.

If these features require the collection, transmission, or sharing of information, we will update this Privacy Policy and the relevant disclosures before or when those features are introduced.

8. Children's Privacy

Day Pilot is intended for general users and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Day Pilot account because the current version does not require account registration.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Day Pilot's features or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available through the Privacy Policy link provided for Day Pilot.
